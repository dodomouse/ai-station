# Video Shotcraft

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) | English | [Import report](../../docs/imports/2026-11-15.en.md)

A product-video workflow that captures real webpages and builds editable Remotion-based timelines and export drafts.

## Use cases

- Create product demonstrations
- Prepare promotional video drafts

## Requirements and limits

Requires Node/npm, FFmpeg/ffprobe, a browser and accessible source pages. Jianying compatibility is version-specific and untested here. Audio assets keep their own licenses; Remotion has separate licensing terms that can require payment. The project's Apache-2.0 license does not cover every dependency or asset.
