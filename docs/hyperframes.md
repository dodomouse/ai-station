## 中文

用 HTML/CSS 和可定位时间的动画制作视频。通过无头浏览器与 FFmpeg 确定性渲染 MP4，提供 CLI、Agent 技能和桌面工作台。

**环境与限制：** 本地工作流需要 Node.js 22+ 与 FFmpeg，并使用 Chromium 渲染。云端模型、托管服务和 Pro 功能的费用与许可独立。

**入门：** 从[官方说明](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/main/README.md)确认安装与配置步骤，再查看[发布版本](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/releases)。本次为文档、许可原文与维护证据审查，未安装运行。

## English

Creates videos from HTML/CSS and seekable animations, rendering MP4 with headless Chromium and FFmpeg through a CLI, agent skills or desktop studio.

**Requirements and limitations:** Local workflows need Node.js 22+ and FFmpeg. Hosted models, cloud services and Pro capabilities have separate pricing and terms.

**Getting started:** Follow the [official documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/main/README.md) for setup, then check [releases](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/releases). This review examined documentation, license text and maintenance evidence; the software was not installed or run.
