## 中文

通过精听、跟读、复述和复习流程练习英语听说。

### 主要功能

- 导入音频后按句练习，记录难句和学习进度。
- 结合语境卡片、回放控制和可选 AI 讲解。

### 环境与限制

- 当前正式产品聚焦 iOS 与 Android，桌面版仍在开发。
- 本地练习可离线，云端讲解与转写涉及配额和可能的会员费用。

## English

An English listening and speaking app organized around intensive listening, shadowing, retelling and review.

### Features

- Practices imported audio sentence by sentence and records difficult passages and progress.
- Combines contextual flashcards, playback controls and optional AI explanations.

### Requirements and limits

- Current releases focus on iOS and Android
- desktop versions remain in development.
- Local practice works offline, while cloud explanations and transcription have quotas and possible membership charges.

### Getting started

Choose the platform or integration in the [official documentation](https://github.com/echo-loop/Echo-Loop/blob/main/README.en.md), review its dependencies and permissions, and follow its quickstart. Check [release records](https://github.com/echo-loop/Echo-Loop/releases) for versions.
