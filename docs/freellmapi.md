## English

Combines the user's own model-provider accounts behind a local gateway with OpenAI-compatible endpoints, routing, failover and quota tracking.

**Requirements and limitations:** Requires Node.js 20+ or Docker and provider accounts. Upstream quotas, regional access and terms still apply. It targets personal prototyping; it is not a promise of unlimited free or production-grade service. Premium catalog features are separate.

**Getting started:** Follow the [official documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tashfeenahmed/freellmapi/main/README.md) for setup, then check [releases](https://github.com/tashfeenahmed/freellmapi/releases). This review examined documentation, license text and maintenance evidence; the software was not installed or run.
