## English

An Android TV launcher with app grids, folders, HDMI shortcuts, wallpaper screensavers and phone-based remote management.

**Requirements and limitations:** Choose the device-appropriate APK and configure the home app through installation or ADB. Boot, focus and HDMI compatibility vary by television or box. The code license does not grant additional rights to Apple videos or third-party icons.

**Getting started:** Follow the [official documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pengchujin/aerial-tv/main/README.md) for setup, then check [releases](https://github.com/pengchujin/aerial-tv/releases). This review examined documentation, license text and maintenance evidence; the software was not installed or run.
