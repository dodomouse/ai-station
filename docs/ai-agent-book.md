An open textbook with companion experiments and skills for learning AI agent design and engineering.

### Features

- Covers context, memory, tools, evaluation and continual improvement.

### Requirements and limits

- Reading does not require a model
- experiments have per-directory Python, model or service requirements.
- Examples are educational, and their results and costs need verification in your environment.
