## 中文

在 macOS 屏幕顶部暂存截图、复制文本和链接，便于拖放与再次使用。

### 主要功能

- 支持预览、标注、置顶、保存和多项选择。
- 可将暂存内容拖到应用或文件夹。

### 环境与限制

- 要求 macOS 14+，支持 Apple 芯片和 Intel。
- 代码采用 MIT，但名称和图标不在代码授权内，改版分发需按许可说明处理品牌素材。

## English

A macOS shelf for screenshots, copied text and links, with drag-and-drop reuse.

### Features

- Supports preview, markup, pinning, saving and multi-selection.
- Moves stored items into apps or folders by drag and drop.

### Requirements and limits

- Requires macOS 14+ on Apple silicon or Intel.
- Code is MIT, while the name and icon are excluded
- modified distributions must follow the branding terms.

### Getting started

Choose the platform or integration in the [official documentation](https://github.com/alejandrobujan/tendedero/blob/main/README.md), review its dependencies and permissions, and follow its quickstart. Check [release records](https://github.com/alejandrobujan/tendedero/releases) for versions.
