## 中文

在交互式三维地球上叠加公开交通、地理与环境数据。

### 主要功能

- 浏览卫星、航班、船舶和其他公开图层。
- 提供街景入口、地图导入及可选语音交互。

### 环境与限制

- 源码需要官方支持的 Node.js 24.x 或 26.x，也可用 Docker。
- MIT 仅覆盖代码，部分数据为非商业许可，外部实时数据还受各供应商条款与账号限制。

## English

An interactive 3D globe combining public transport, geographic and environmental data.

### Features

- Explores satellite, flight, vessel and other public layers.
- Provides street-level imagery entry points, map imports and optional voice interaction.

### Requirements and limits

- Source setup needs a supported Node.js 24.x or 26.x release, or Docker.
- MIT covers code only
- some datasets are noncommercial, and live providers impose their own terms and account requirements.

### Getting started

Choose the platform or integration in the [official documentation](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view/blob/main/README.md), review its dependencies and permissions, and follow its quickstart. Check [release records](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view/releases) for versions.
